VELIRRA ALLURE

Inspired by Versace Crystal Bright

₨ 2,350

Velirra Allure is a fresh, feminine fragrance inspired by Versace Crystal Bright. Crisp citrus and sparkling fruit open into a delicate floral heart of peony, magnolia and lotus flower, before settling into a soft, musky woody base — clean, elegant and made to last.

Longevity 8+ hrs on skin
Concentration Eau de Parfum 50ML
Best Season Spring & Summer

FRAGRANCE NOTES

Top Notes – Opening

Yuzu · Iced Accord · Pomegranate

Fresh, juicy, citrusy, fruity and slightly sparkling.

Heart Notes – Middle

Peony · Magnolia · Lotus Flower

Soft, feminine, airy, elegant and beautifully floral.

Base Notes – Dry Down

Acajou (Mahogany) · Vegetal Amber · Musk

Soft woody warmth, gentle amber and clean musky sweetness.
